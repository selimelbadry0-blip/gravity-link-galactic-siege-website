How to Play

Master the Gravity Link

In Gravity Link: Galactic Siege, you control advanced gravity technology to:

  • Create gravitational fields to move objects and enemies
  • Link gravity points to solve complex puzzles
  • Navigate through dangerous space environments
  • Defeat enemies using gravity-based attacks
  • Unlock new abilities as you progress
